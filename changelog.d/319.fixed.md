- **A demotion that was held back no longer undoes a newer change of the preferred leader.** A
  cosigner that says it lacks a key loses the lead only when a later cosigner serves the call,
  which can be seconds after its answer. If another call moved the preferred leader in between,
  the late demotion could move it again, onto a cosigner that call had just found unreachable,
  and take a cosigner that had just signed out of the sign rotation. The demotion is now applied
  as old as it is: it moves the lead only if nothing has moved it since the cosigner answered,
  and the time it records is the time of that answer. (Unreleased behaviour; no configuration
  change.)
