- **A demotion that was held back no longer undoes a newer change of the preferred leader.** A
  cosigner that says it lacks a key loses the lead only when a later cosigner serves the call,
  which can be seconds after its answer. If another call moved the preferred leader in between,
  the late demotion could move it again, also onto a cosigner that call had just found
  unreachable; and so could the step that makes the cosigner that served the preferred leader.
  Both now move the lead only if nothing has moved it since the call saw it, and the demotion
  records the time of the cosigner's answer, not the time the call ended. (Unreleased behaviour;
  no configuration change.)
