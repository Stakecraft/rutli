- **CI checks that each self-hosted runner's installed job-started guard is the repository's.**
  `scripts/check-runner-guard.sh`, a step of the deploy-policy job, compares the hook the runner
  runs (`ACTIONS_RUNNER_HOOK_JOB_STARTED`) with `deploy/ci-runner/job-started-guard.sh` and names the
  runner when they differ: a warning on a pull request, a failure on a push to main.
