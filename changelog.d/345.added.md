- **The cosigner as a release binary**: from the next release the assets include
  `rutli-cosigner-<version>-linux-x86_64` and `-linux-aarch64`, for hosts without a container
  runtime. They go through the same steps as the dealer binary: built on GitHub-hosted runners from
  the tag, listed in `SHA256SUMS`, signed with cosign (keyless) and attested, and verified by the
  release job before the upload (`docs/releasing.md`, "Verify the release assets"). They are built
  on `ubuntu-22.04` and need glibc 2.35 or newer (Debian 12 and 13); the
  release job fails if a build needs a newer one. The SBOM of the Rust workspace
  (`rutli-dealer-<version>.spdx.json`) covers them. The container image is unchanged and stays the
  default.
