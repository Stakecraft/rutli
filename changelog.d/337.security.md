- **The status RPCs apply the per-key ACL to the driver role.** `KeyStatus`, `HostedKeyStatus` and
  `CeremonySignTest` checked the caller's role and pin only. A certificate on `signer_allowlist` is
  now held to its `signing_policy.signer_acl` entry on these three RPCs as on `Sign` and `Dkg`,
  `signer_acl_required` included, and a `CeremonySignTest` the ACL refuses takes nothing from the
  key's sign-test budget. The operator role still reads every key, and the refresher role keeps
  `KeyStatus` for every key. A driver certificate without a `signer_acl` entry reads every key while
  `signer_acl_required` is off, as before, and none once it is on. Upgrade note: `verify-key`,
  `verify-hosted` and `key show` run with a driver certificate now follow that certificate's ACL;
  for a key it does not cover, run them with the operator certificate (`operator_allowlist`).
